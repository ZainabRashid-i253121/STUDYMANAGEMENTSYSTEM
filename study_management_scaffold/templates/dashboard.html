How to use this dashboard

  • Your tasks and their deadlines are listed below, earliest deadline first.
  • A task with no deadline stays in the list and is marked Missing deadline information.
  • Use Mark complete when a task is done. Its deadline is kept. Use Mark incomplete to undo it.
  • Use the All / Incomplete / Completed links to filter the list.
  • The progress counts above the list update automatically.
