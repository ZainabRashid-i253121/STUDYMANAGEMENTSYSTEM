# S07 — User Availability and Study Schedule

## Requirements

- PRD FR-06 / AC-FR-06.1–06.2.
- PRD FR-16 / AC-FR-16.1–16.3.
- Plan-v03 S07; architecture §§5–8; ADR-005 and ADR-006.

## User-selected scheduling rules

- Each task has a user-entered study-duration estimate in whole minutes.
- Tasks with higher Priority are allocated first. Equal-priority tasks use nearest deadline first; if both priority and deadline are equal, the Task Management service's existing list order is retained.
- Sessions are placed chronologically inside the user's saved weekly availability periods.
- A task may span multiple availability periods. Its sessions are persisted only if the full estimate can be placed on or before its deadline.
- Tasks are not overbooked. Tasks without an estimate, deadline, or determinable Priority, and tasks that do not fit by their deadline, remain unscheduled.
- The planning horizon begins at the supplied/current date and ends at the latest eligible deadline. Tasks with `Undetermined` Priority remain unscheduled because the service supplies no rank.
- Completed tasks are excluded. Overlapping saved availability periods are merged so time is not counted twice. Existing future scheduled sessions are preserved and treated as occupied time; their duration counts toward the task's estimate on repeated generation. Schedule adjustment is S08 scope.

## Implementation

- `Task_Management.update_study_duration` validates and stores user-provided estimates on owned task records.
- `Data_Access.init_db()` adds the nullable `study_duration_minutes` field to existing databases and includes it in legacy task-table rebuilds.
- `Schedule.update_availability` updates an owned weekly period. `Schedule.generate_study_schedule` uses Task Management and Priority interfaces and persists generated sessions transactionally.
- Authenticated routes and the schedule page support availability entry/update, duration entry on the dashboard, schedule generation, and viewing scheduled sessions with task titles.
